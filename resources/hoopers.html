About NITA Hoopers

NITA Hoopers is the official basketball club of NIT Agartala, dedicated to promoting the sport of basketball within the college community. The club organizes regular practice sessions, friendly matches, and participates in inter-college tournaments. It serves as a platform for students to enhance their basketball skills, foster teamwork, and build a strong sports culture on campus.
